ASTM C136 / AASHTO T27

1. Sieve Analysis & Particle Size Distribution

Sieve Stack: 2" to #200 Shaking: 10–15 min Tolerance: ≤ 0.3%
Engineering Objective:

Determine the quantitative particle size distribution of coarse and fine aggregates to verify compliance with AASHTO M 147 grading envelopes and compute uniformity (Cu) and curvature (Cc) coefficients.

1

Oven-dry representative aggregate sample at 110 ± 5°C to constant mass and record initial total dry weight (Wtotal).

2

Wash over No. 200 (0.075 mm) sieve (AASHTO T11) if required, re-dry, and nest sieves from largest opening at top to pan at bottom.

3

Agitate sieve nest in mechanical shaker for 10–15 minutes until less than 0.5% passes any sieve during 1 minute of hand sieving.

4

Weigh material retained on each sieve, compute % Passing, interpolate D10, D30, D60, and check upper/lower spec limits.

ASTM C131 / AASHTO T96

2. Los Angeles Abrasion Resistance Test

Drum: Ø 711 × 508 mm Speed: 30–33 rpm Revolutions: 500
Engineering Objective:

Evaluate coarse aggregate toughness and resistance to degradation by abrasion, impact, and grinding in a rotating steel drum containing standard abrasive steel spheres.

1

Prepare clean, oven-dried aggregate grading (A, B, C, or D) totaling 5,000 ± 10 g (W1) and select corresponding charge of steel spheres.

2

Place aggregate sample and steel spheres inside the Los Angeles testing drum and secure the dust-tight cover.

3

Rotate drum at 30 to 33 rpm for 500 revolutions; internal shelf lifts and drops sample and balls each turn.

4

Discharge material, sieve over No. 12 (1.70 mm) sieve, wash retained portion, oven-dry to constant mass (W2), and compute % Loss.

ASTM D1557 / AASHTO T180

3. Modified Proctor Compaction Test (MDD & OMC)

Rammer: 4.54 kg (10 lb) Drop: 457 mm (18 in) 5 Layers × 56 Blows
Engineering Objective:

Establish the moisture-density relationship under high compactive effort (2,700 kN-m/m³) to determine Maximum Dry Density (MDD) and Optimum Moisture Content (OMC) for field compaction control.

1

Prepare 5 representative soil/aggregate specimens mixed at increasing water contents bracketing expected OMC.

2

Compact each specimen in 5 equal layers using a 4.54 kg rammer dropped from 457 mm (56 blows/layer in 152.4 mm mold).

3

Trim specimen flush with mold top, weigh compacted soil + mold to find wet bulk density (γwet), and take moisture tins.

4

Compute γdry = γwet / (1 + w), plot compaction curve alongside Zero Air Voids (ZAV) line, and extract MDD & OMC.

ASTM D1883 / AASHTO T193

4. California Bearing Ratio (Soaked CBR) Test

Piston Area: 1,935 mm² (Ø 49.6 mm) Rate: 1.27 mm/min Soaking: 96 hrs (4 Days)
Engineering Objective:

Evaluate the bearing strength of compacted base, subbase, and subgrade materials after 96-hour soaking by comparing penetration stress at 2.5 mm and 5.0 mm against standard crushed stone values (6.9 MPa and 10.3 MPa).

1

Compact 3 specimens at OMC using 10, 30, and 65 blows/layer (5 layers) to bracket target field density (95% or 98% MDD).

2

Place annular surcharge weights (≥ 4.54 kg) and immerse molds in water for 96 hours while monitoring swell dial gauge.

3

Drain 15 min, apply seating load, and penetrate piston at 1.27 mm/min while recording load readings up to 12.5 mm.

4

Apply initial concavity correction if curve is upward-concave, compute CBR at 2.5 & 5.0 mm, and interpolate design CBR at target compaction.

ASTM D4318 / AASHTO T89 & T90

5. Atterberg Consistency Limits (Liquid Limit & Plasticity Index)

Fraction: Passing No. 40 (0.425 mm) Cup Drop: 10 mm @ 2 drops/sec PL Thread: Ø 3.2 mm
Engineering Objective:

Quantify the plasticity and moisture sensitivity of the fine fraction passing the No. 40 (0.425 mm) sieve to prevent excessive shrink-swell volume changes and loss of shear strength.

1

Mix soil passing No. 40 sieve with distilled water into a uniform paste and place in the brass Casagrande cup.

2

Cut standard groove and turn crank at 2 drops/sec until groove closes along 13 mm; record blows N and moisture w%.

3

Roll ellipsoidal soil mass on frosted glass plate into a 3.2 mm (1/8") thread until it crumbles to determine Plastic Limit (PL).

4

Regress semi-log Flow Curve at N = 25 blows for LL, calculate PI = LL − PL, and plot on Casagrande A-Line chart.

AASHTO M 145 / ASTM D2487 (USCS)

6. Automated Engineering Soil & Aggregate Classification

AASHTO: A-1-a to A-7-6 Group Index (GI) USCS: GW–MH
Engineering Objective:

Automatically classify highway soils and unbound aggregates under both AASHTO M 145 (with Group Index GI) and the Unified Soil Classification System (USCS - ASTM D2487) using live sieve and Atterberg test data.

1

Extract cumulative percent passing No. 10 (2.00 mm), No. 40 (0.425 mm), and No. 200 (0.075 mm) directly from Sieve Analysis.

2

Apply left-to-right elimination across AASHTO M 145 table from A-1-a through A-7-6 and evaluate Group Index (GI).

3

Check USCS 50% fines boundary, gravel vs. sand split at No. 4 (4.75 mm), and well-graded criteria (Cu & Cc).

4

Evaluate position relative to Casagrande A-Line (PI = 0.73(LL − 20)) to assign dual or fine-grained symbols.

AASHTO T 307 / AASHTO 1993

7. Resilient Modulus (Mr) & Structural Layer Coefficients

Cyclic Haversine: 0.1 s Load / 0.9 s Rest Bulk Stress Model: k1 · (θ/Pa)^k2
Engineering Objective:

Determine the elastic stiffness (Resilient Modulus Mr = σd / εr) under repeated traffic wheel pulses and derive AASHTO 1993 structural layer coefficients (a2, a3) for pavement thickness design.

1

Mount cylindrical specimen inside rubber membrane in triaxial cell and attach twin axial LVDT displacement transducers.

2

Apply conditioning sequence followed by multi-stage confining pressures (σ3) and cyclic deviator stresses (σd).

3

Compute recoverable axial strain (εr), bulk stress (θ = σd + 3σ3), and Mr = σd / εr for each stress state.

4

Fit non-linear power model Mr = k1(θ/Pa)k2, compare against CBR correlation, and compute a2 or a3.

8. Flexible Pavement Layer Thickness Calculator (Structural Number SN)

Engineering Objective:

Solve the AASHTO 1993 Flexible Pavement Design Equation iteratively for required Structural Numbers (SN1, SN2, SN3) and optimize practical layer thicknesses (D1, D2, D3) in centimeters and inches.

1

Specify design traffic (W18 ESALs), reliability level (R%), standard deviation (So), and serviceability loss (ΔPSI).

2

Enter or sync Resilient Moduli for Asphalt (EAC), Base (EBS), Subbase (ESB), and Subgrade (Mr).

3

Solve required SN1 (above Base), SN2 (above Subbase), and total SN3 (above Subgrade).

4

Round layer thicknesses to practical construction increments (0.5 cm) and verify SN* ≥ SN at every interface.
